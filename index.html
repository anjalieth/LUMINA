About Us

Redefining the Student Journey

Lumina is a premier study abroad consultancy dedicated to bridging the gap between traditional education and global exploration. Founded on the belief that the world’s most profound lessons are learned outside the classroom, Lumina curates immersive academic experiences in the most inspiring cultural hubs—from the historic streets of Florence to the high-tech skylines of Seoul.

We don't just facilitate visas and university placements; we design life-changing journeys. By focusing on high-quality programs and personal student growth, Lumina empowers the next generation of global citizens to expand their horizons, master new languages, and build a professional network that spans continents. At Lumina, we believe that when you change your environment, you change your future.
